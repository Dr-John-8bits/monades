MUTABLE DREAMS 1.1
Private release copy — publish only after final package and installer acceptance.

Thank you for the response to Mutable Dreams, and for the recordings, ideas and
careful feedback. This update focuses on warmth, personal Biomes and a more
complete instrument for ambient music, bass lines and evolving rhythms.

Six freely assignable tracks now offer twelve sound engines, 224 factory tones
and 76 musical Biomes, plus Blank. Explore Round Bass, Elements Strike, Mellow
Drift and Satin Morph alongside the selected Plaits/Rings-based voices. Shape
the result with filters and envelopes, per-track mixer sends, a granular bus,
shared delay and room, and Marbles/Tides/Warps-inspired helpers.

Write your own sixteen-step phrases. Save sixteen named User Biomes and
sixty-four User Sounds. Play the six tracks over USB MIDI, or synchronize to an
external MIDI clock. Organic motion, clearer controls and held-key help make
the new pages easier to explore.

The browser instrument also works independently. Connect FM-1 opens the live
hardware editor, with individual JSON Biome exchange and complete .mddb bank
backup/restore. Browser-local banks and hardware banks are separate.

The direct update from Mutable Dreams 1.0 and the Stock V15 roundtrip have been
checked on the actual FM-1 with the documented candidate. Read the matching
backup guide first. Returning to V15 erases new banks/preferences; restoring them
after reinstalling 1.1 needs the .mddb file and physical SEL confirmation.

With gratitude to Émilie Gillet / Mutable Instruments and the platform and
open-source contributors. Selected original algorithms and inspired helpers;
not full replicas of every Mutable module. An independent community project.
The published 1.0 remains available and unchanged.
